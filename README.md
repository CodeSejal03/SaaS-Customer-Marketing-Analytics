# 📊 SaaS Customer & Marketing Analytics

## Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end analysis of a SaaS business using Power BI, combining customer analytics, churn analysis, customer engagement, marketing performance, and competitor intelligence.

The objective is to help business teams understand customer behavior, identify churn risks, evaluate acquisition efficiency, monitor engagement, and make data-driven marketing and retention decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

A SaaS company needs to answer several important business questions:

- How large and healthy is the current customer base?
- Which subscription plans experience the highest churn?
- How does customer engagement differ between active and churned users?
- Which marketing channels acquire customers most efficiently?
- Where should marketing budget be increased or optimized?
- How does the company compare with competitors across pricing, features, ratings, and market positioning?

This Power BI solution brings these areas together into one decision-support system.

## 👥 Customer Analysis

The Customer Analysis dashboard evaluates customer distribution, churn, subscription plans, industries, and satisfaction.

### Key Findings

- Total customer base is approximately **2,000 customers**.
- **295 customers have churned**.
- Overall customer churn rate is **14.8%**.
- Average NPS score is **8.9**.
- The **Starter plan has the highest churn rate**, at approximately 20%.
- Enterprise customers demonstrate the lowest churn among the three plans.
- Enterprise customers also report the strongest average NPS.
- Healthcare and Retail represent some of the largest customer industries in the dataset.

### Business Interpretation

Starter-plan customers represent an important retention opportunity. Their higher churn suggests that onboarding, product adoption, perceived value, pricing, or upgrade pathways should be investigated further.

## 💡 Customer Engagement Analysis

This dashboard analyzes product usage and engagement behavior.

### Engagement KPIs

| Metric | Value |
|---|---:|
| Average NPS Score | 8.9 |
| Email Open Rate | 75.3% |
| Average Monthly Logins | 22.5 |
| Average Features Used | 6.6 |

### Key Findings

- Monthly login activity shows an overall upward trend.
- Active customers log in more frequently than churned customers.
- Active customers also demonstrate higher NPS scores.
- Enterprise customers use the highest number of product features.
- Starter customers use considerably fewer features.

### Business Interpretation

The relationship between lower product engagement and churn suggests that **product adoption can act as an important retention indicator**.

Customers with declining login frequency or limited feature adoption could be targeted with onboarding campaigns, product education, feature recommendations, and proactive retention initiatives.

## 📣 Marketing Campaign Performance

This dashboard evaluates marketing spend, acquisition cost, conversions, CTR, and lead conversion performance across channels.

### Marketing KPIs

| Metric | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Marketing Spend | $310.431K |
| Customer Acquisition Cost | $155.22 |
| Click-Through Rate | 2.50% |
| Lead Conversion Rate | 14.0% |

### Channel Insights

- **Paid Search** receives the highest marketing spend.
- Paid Search also has the highest customer acquisition cost.
- **Organic Search generates the highest total conversions**.
- **LinkedIn Ads shows the strongest lead conversion rate**, at approximately 26%.
- Referral and Webinar channels show comparatively low acquisition costs.
- Channel performance demonstrates that higher marketing spend does not automatically produce better acquisition efficiency.

### Business Recommendation

Marketing budget should not be allocated based only on conversion volume.

Management should evaluate:

**Conversion Volume + Conversion Rate + CAC + Customer Quality**

together when deciding future channel investments.

High-performing channels such as LinkedIn Ads, Referral, and Organic Search should be investigated for potential budget scaling, while expensive channels such as Paid Search should be continuously evaluated for efficiency.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

The combined analysis indicates several important opportunities:

1. **Starter customers require greater retention attention** because they show the highest churn and lower product adoption.

2. **Product engagement is closely associated with customer health.** Active customers demonstrate stronger login activity and satisfaction than churned customers.

3. **Organic Search is an important acquisition engine**, generating strong customer and conversion volume.

4. **Paid Search requires efficiency monitoring** because it combines high spend with comparatively high acquisition cost.

5. **LinkedIn Ads demonstrates strong lead conversion performance**, making it a potential candidate for further investment testing.

6. **Enterprise customers show stronger engagement and satisfaction**, suggesting opportunities to identify behaviors that can be encouraged across lower-tier plans.

7. Competitor positioning shows that **analytics, automation, AI capabilities, and customer intelligence** are important differentiators in the SaaS market.

## 💼 Recommended Business Actions

### 1. Build an Early Churn Intervention Strategy

Monitor indicators such as:

- declining login frequency
- low feature adoption
- falling NPS
- low campaign engagement

Customers showing multiple warning signals can be prioritized for retention campaigns.

### 2. Improve Starter Plan Adoption

Introduce:

- structured onboarding
- product walkthroughs
- feature education
- lifecycle emails
- upgrade recommendations

to improve adoption and reduce churn.

### 3. Optimize Marketing Budget Allocation

Shift decision-making from simply measuring spend toward:

**CAC + Conversion Rate + Conversion Volume + Customer Value**

This allows marketing teams to identify channels that generate sustainable customer growth.

### 4. Replicate High-Value Customer Behaviors

Analyze the engagement patterns of Enterprise customers and encourage similar feature adoption among Professional and Starter users.

### 5. Use Competitive Intelligence for Positioning

Monitor competitor pricing, free trials, features, ratings, and positioning to identify opportunities for product differentiation.
